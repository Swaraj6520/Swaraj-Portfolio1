The problem

You spend to get the lead. Then the part that decides the deal happens out of sight.

Your CRM can tell you whether a job closed. It usually can’t tell you what happened at the kitchen table, which objection changed the conversation, or why one rep keeps winning the same deals everyone else loses.

01

Ride-alongs don’t scale

A manager can shadow a few reps. They can’t sit in on every inspection, estimate, and follow-up happening across the week.

02

“I think it went well” isn’t enough

Post-call notes depend on memory. The useful part is often buried in the actual conversation, not the recap afterward.

03

Your best reps know things the team doesn’t

The way they frame price, handle insurance questions, or earn trust can stay stuck in their heads instead of becoming a repeatable playbook.

How it works

Listen. Learn. Coach the moments that actually matter.

COACH turns everyday customer conversations into something managers can use. Less guessing, fewer random ride-alongs, and more specific coaching.
