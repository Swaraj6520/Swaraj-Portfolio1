Writing Sample · Product Marketing

From a raw changelog to a launch people actually read.

I haven't shipped at Retell yet. So I took one of your recent releases, Live Call Monitoring, and rewrote it the way I'd announce it. The raw engineering note first, my version second, and then the part that matters most: why anyone should care.

Before — the raw changelog
release_notes.md 
v2.6 — Live Call Monitoring (GA)
- Real-time dashboard of all active/in-progress agent calls
- Live transcript streaming per call (sub-second latency)
- Status indicators: active / escalating / flagged
- Configurable alerts: trigger phrases, sentiment drop, silence timeout
- Barge-in: supervisor can join or take over a live call
- Filter/search active calls by agent, campaign, phone number
- WebSocket API to pipe live call state to external dashboards
- Deprecates post-call-only review flow as sole QA path
After — the announcement I'd ship

Live Call Monitoring is here! Watch every call as it happens and step in before it goes wrong.

A bad call used to be a post-mortem. You’d read the transcript after the customer had already hung up, being frustrated and already gone. Too late to do anything but wince.

Now you watch it live.

Live Call Monitoring gives you a real-time view of every conversation your agents are having right now. See one going sideways, and jump in before it goes wrong.

  • Every live call, one screen. Watch each active conversation stream in real-time, transcript and all. No refresh, no waiting for the recording.
  • Catch trouble the second it starts. Set Alerts on the signals that matter. Like a frustrated tone, a trigger phrase, or a long silence, this will get flagged the instant a call needs your attention.
  • Step in without missing a beat. Join a live call or take it over entirely. Your customer never feels the handoff.
  • Find the one that matters, fast. Filter active calls by agent, campaign, or number, and go straight to the conversation that needs your eyes & ears.

The save was always in the moment, not the recap. Now your team can actually be there for it, across every call, at once.

See what your agents are saying, right now. Settings → Monitoring. Live today.
Why it matters — the customer value

Oversight goes from forensic to real-time.

For a business running voice AI at scale, the scariest moment isn't a bad call, it's a bad call nobody saw until the customer was already gone. As volume grows, reviewing recordings after the fact stops working: you're auditing a fraction, always in hindsight, always too late to save the relationship. Live Call Monitoring lets a small team supervise thousands of simultaneous conversations, catch the ones going wrong while they can still be fixed, and step in before a frustrated caller becomes a churned one. For any company betting real relationships on voice AI, that shift from reviewing failures to preventing them is what makes scaling the automation safe.

The thinking
01

Lead with the pain, not the feature.

The raw note opens on a dashboard. The announcement opens on the moment a call goes wrong and you can't see it, you feel the old problem before the fix arrives.

02

Every bullet is an outcome, not a spec.

"Configurable alerts" becomes "catch trouble the second it starts." Same fact, reframed around what the customer actually gets.

03

Punchy launch, measured value note.

The announcement moves fast; the value note slows down to make the business case. Knowing which register fits where is the job.
