Writing Sample · Brand Narrative

Making an AI adversary feel human.

Antigen is an offensive-security startup, a dense, technical story that's easy to make scary and easy to ignore. They didn't ask for this; I took their public positioning and rewrote it as a spec piece. They didn't ask for this; I made it. I took their public positioning and rewrote it the way I'd tell their story: confident, human, and about relief instead of fear.

Before — the positioning today

Antigen is an AI adversary for the enterprise. We provide continuous offensive security — mapping, exploiting, and securing your attack surface through nightly automated offensive testing and monthly human red-team assessments. Every finding ships with traces, reproduction steps, and remediation, delivered to GitHub, Linear, or the Antigen Platform. State-of-the-art on public benchmarks. Trusted across financial services, healthcare, retail, and government.

Accurate. Credible. And forgettable to anyone who isn't already a security buyer — it says what they do, never why it matters.

After — the human version

The only way to know you're safe is to be attacked first, by someone on your side.

Every company runs on software it hopes is secure. "Hopes" is the problem.

Because somewhere out there, someone is already trying the locks, patient, creative, and no longer alone. AI has handed every attacker a tireless partner. What used to take a skilled hacker days now takes an afternoon, and the person on the other end doesn't need to be skilled at all. They just need a tool and a reason.

So finding out where you're weak after a breach, in the headlines, isn't a strategy. It's a countdown.

Antigen flips it. We're the attacker who's on your side. Every night, our AI adversary does to your systems exactly what a real one would, maps the way in, finds the crack, and proves it. Not a vague "you might be vulnerable," but the exact steps, so your team can see it, fix it, and move on. Once a month, real people go deeper. You don't get a frightening report. You get the thing every security team actually wants: the problem, found and closed, before anyone else finds it.

Financial services, healthcare, retail, government, the places where a breach isn't an inconvenience but a broken promise, trust us to break in first.

Security was never about hoping the door holds. It's about someone you trust trying the handle, every single night.

The thinking
01

Sold confidence, not fear.

Security marketing defaults to anxiety. The more human, and more differentiated, story is relief: we're not here to scare you, we're here to let you stop worrying.

02

Made the abstract tangible.

"Offensive security" is a concept. "Someone you trust trying the handle every night" is an image you can feel. Human stories run on images, not jargon.

03

Named the stakes, then resolved them.

The piece admits the scary new reality, AI arms every attacker, then turns it into the reason Antigen exists. Tension, then release. That arc keeps people reading.
