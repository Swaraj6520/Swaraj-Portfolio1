Spree: two reels, one engine.

How I turn a one-line idea into a 9:16 reel with Google Flow / Veo, and where a human stays in the loop.

Role · Concept to publish Tools · Google Flow / Veo Format · 9:16 vertical Review · Human on every cut
The idea

Sell the feeling. Let the product be the punchline.

Spree is a personalized deal-feed app. Deals are hard to make interesting: a price is a number, and numbers don't stop a scroll.

The feeling around a deal is much more interesting. The 3 AM rush when the alert you set finally fires. The quiet "worth it" after you order. So both reels start from a human moment, and the app shows up as the reason for it.

Everything below is AI-generated, including the people. Same engine, two different moods.

Under the hood

What the prompt is actually doing.

01

A defined first frame

Every clip opens on a specific image: mid-shake in the 3 AM reel, phone glow on a face in the other. The hook is decided before generating, not discovered after.

02

Characters locked in words

The same descriptors repeat in both clips (freckles, messy bun, glasses, light stubble), so the model keeps the same people from one clip to the next.

03

A handoff between clips

Clip B "opens on his alarmed face, matching the previous frame." Each clip ends on a named frame so the cut feels like one take.

04

Emotion written as beats

Alarm, processing, flat, "go back to sleep." The performance is directed like a script, and that is where the comedy comes from.

05

Light with a job

A dark room and one saturated color: the hot-pink phone glow. The product is the brightest thing in the frame.

06

Captions written with the script

Subtitles are scripted next to the dialogue, so the story reads with the sound off. That is how most people will watch it.

The workflow

From idea to post, with a human in the middle.

01

Concept

Start from a feeling, not a feature. What does a deal alert feel like at 3 AM?

02

Script

Lines, subtitles and the last frame get written before anything is generated.

03

Generate

Google Flow / Veo, in two short clips so faces and mood stay consistent.

04 · Human

Review

A person checks the faces, the pacing, the joke and the voice before it goes anywhere.

05

Edit & publish

Stitch the clips, add captions, post.

06

Learn

Audience response decides the next idea.

Key insight: AI is fast at pixels, not at taste. The prompt is the creative brief, and the review is the edit.

Both reels are fully AI-generated with Google Flow / Veo. No real people appear. The prompts above are the exact ones used.
